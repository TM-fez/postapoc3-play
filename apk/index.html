ASHFALL – alpha 2 (native Godot, Android)

New in alpha 2: every house, shack, barn, workshop, shop, apartment block and Haven building is now a hollow, enterable shell with door and window openings (frames, glass / boarded / broken windows), doors you open and close with the interact button, floors, ceilings, internal rooms, stairs in multi-storey buildings, furniture (beds, tables, shelves, cabinets...), lights inside at night, lootable containers, and a few locked doors that need a key or a lockpick (keys are hidden in crates/trunks). Ground now uses 1K CC0 PBR textures (ambientCG: grass, dirt, rock, sand, asphalt) with normal maps, splat blending, macro variation and mipmaps, plus grass tufts, roads with markings, fences, signs, wrecks, barrels, lamps, more trees and bushes. Filmic tonemap, shadows, sky ambient/fog tuning, vertex AO darkening. Credits are bundled in the game (Settings).

arm64 Android 7+, debug-keystore signed, not yet tested on a device (only headless / software-GL tests).
